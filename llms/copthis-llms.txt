# CopThis (Merchbar)

> CopThis is the company behind Merchbar, the official online merchandise retailer for musicians and artists. CopThis operates the Merchbar Partner API — a REST contract that merchandise and fulfillment partners implement so Merchbar can list a partner's stores and merchandise and place, track, update, and cancel orders on behalf of Merchbar customers. HTTP Basic auth over HTTPS, a JSON `data` envelope with pagination, and URI-path versioning (v1).
